💖 HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN PHOTOS TO THIS WEBSITE 💖
==============================================================

It's super easy! You have two simple options:

OPTION A (Easiest - Just replace the files):
1. Copy your 6 favorite photos into this "images" folder.
2. Rename them to:
   - photo1.jpg
   - photo2.jpg
   - photo3.jpg
   - photo4.jpg
   - photo5.jpg
   - photo6.jpg
3. In index.html, change ".svg" to ".jpg" for the images (e.g., images/photo1.jpg).
   (Or the website will even detect and load .jpg/.png if you want!)

OPTION B (Use any filename or PNG / JPEG / WEBP):
1. Copy your photos into this "images" folder with any name, e.g., "our_paris_trip.jpg".
2. Open "index.html" in Notepad or any code editor.
3. Find the <!-- PHOTO GALLERY --> section.
4. Replace "images/photo1.svg" with "images/our_paris_trip.jpg" and change the caption to whatever you want!

RECOMMENDED PHOTO ORIENTATION:
- Square (1:1) or Portrait (4:5 / 3:4) looks best in the romantic frames.
- High resolution or phone camera photos work beautifully!
